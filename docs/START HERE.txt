REFERRAL DASH - START HERE
==========================

Referral Dash signs in to Tableau for you, downloads your mission's referrals,
and makes area, district, zone and mission dashboards plus District Summaries.
It can upload every run to a Google Drive folder.

FIRST TIME (about 5 minutes)
1. Right-click the ZIP > Extract All. Open the new folder.
2. Double-click "Referral Dash.exe". The app opens in its own window.
   (Windows may ask you to confirm the first time: More info > Run anyway.)
3. Click Settings.
   - Mission: type your mission name EXACTLY as Tableau's Mission filter shows
     it (for example "Tennessee Knoxville Mission"), pick the time zone, Save.
   - Church account: your missionary email, Church username and password.
     They are saved in Windows Credential Manager on this computer only.
   - Google Drive (optional): Connect Google Drive, sign in, then paste the
     link of the Drive folder where reports should go and click Save folder.
4. Optional: click "Try with sample data" to see what the reports look like.

EVERY WEEK
Open Referral Dash and click Run now. Wait for "Reports are ready".
Click "Open folder" or "Open in Drive".

District Summaries compare automatically with last week's run (the newest run
at least five days older). The very first run has nothing to compare with yet.

WHERE THINGS ARE KEPT
- Reports: Documents\Referral Dash reports (change it on the Run page).
- Settings and comparison history: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Referral Dash
  (kept when you install a newer version).

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- "Tableau has no mission named ...": fix the mission name in Settings.
- Sign-in problems: check the Church account in Settings, then turn on
  "Show the browser while downloading" under Tableau connection and run again
  to watch what happens.
- No Chrome or Edge window can open: install or update Microsoft Edge.
- Already downloaded a CSV yourself? Click "Use a CSV I downloaded".
