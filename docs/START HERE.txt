REFERRAL DASH - START HERE
==========================

Referral Dash signs in to Tableau for you, downloads your mission's referrals,
and makes area, district, zone and mission dashboards plus District Summaries.
Everything runs on this computer.

FIRST TIME (about 2 minutes)
1. Right-click the ZIP > Extract All. Open the new folder.
2. Double-click "Referral Dash.exe". The app opens in its own window.
   (Windows may ask you to confirm the first time: More info > Run anyway.)
3. The app asks for two things:
   - Church account: your missionary email, Church username and password.
     They are saved in Windows Credential Manager on this computer only.
   - Mission: your mission name the way Tableau lists it (for example
     "Tennessee Knoxville Mission"). The time zone is filled in from this
     computer; change it if your mission is somewhere else.
   Want a preview first? Click "Try sample data".

EVERY WEEK
Open Referral Dash and click Run now. Wait for "Reports are ready", then
click "Open reports".

District Summaries show each area's growth or decline compared with the week
before, starting with your very first run.

CHOOSING DATES
Reports cover the last 30 days ending today, compared with the same dates 7 days
before. The main screen shows the dates in the line above Run now. To change
them, open Settings > Advanced:
- Reports cover: the past so many days, or a date range.
- Compare to: so many days before, or a date range.
For August 8 to September 8 compared 7 days before, the arrows compare with
August 1 to September 1. A past range shows numbers as they stood on its end
date. A date range stays set until you change it back.

WHERE REPORTS GO
Each run makes a folder named for its dates, like
"Referral Reports - Oct 7 to Nov 6, 2026".
- Reports are saved in Documents\Referral Dash reports. Click "Change" on the
  main screen to save them somewhere else. To share them, pick a folder that
  Google Drive for desktop, OneDrive or a shared drive already syncs.
- Only the newest 8 report sets are kept; older ones are deleted after each
  run so the folder stays small. Type a different number (0 keeps them all) on the
  main screen. Deleting old reports never affects the comparison.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- "Tableau has no mission named ...": it suggests the closest name. Click
  "Fix mission name", correct it, and run again.
- Sign-in problems: check the Church account in Settings, then click
  "Try again and show the browser" to watch what happens.
- Authenticator prompts: a browser window opens by itself; approve the
  sign-in there and Referral Dash carries on.
- No Chrome or Edge window can open: install or update Microsoft Edge.
- Already downloaded a CSV yourself? Click "Use a CSV I downloaded". To get
  the change arrows, choose a date range in Settings > Advanced, and make sure
  your CSV also covers the dates you compare with.
- Settings > Advanced has the rarely needed options (report dates and comparison,
  response-time hours, icons, zones in another time zone).
