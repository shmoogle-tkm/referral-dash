Privacy policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Referral Dash is a Windows app that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what it handles and where that information goes.

1. Information the app handles

When you run it, the app downloads your mission's referral list from Tableau using your account, or reads a file you choose. That list includes the names of people who were referred, their assigned areas, and dates of contact and teaching activity. The app uses it to make dashboard pictures and district summaries, and those pictures can include the names of referrals who have not yet been contacted.

You also enter a mission name, report settings, and the Church account used to sign in to Tableau.

2. Where it is stored

Everything stays on your computer unless you choose to upload it. Reports are saved in the folder you choose. Settings and recent downloads are kept in your Windows user profile. Your Church account and your Google Drive connection are kept in Windows Credential Manager for your Windows user.

The app has no server. Nothing is sent to the people who make Referral Dash, and the app has no advertising or analytics.

3. Google user data

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you connect, the app requests one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. It covers the folder you pick in Google's folder picker and the files and folders the app creates there. The app cannot see or change anything else in your Drive.

The app uses this access only to read the picked folder's name and whether you can add to it, to upload your report pictures into it, and to move the copy it uploaded the previous time to the Drive trash. Google gives the app a token for this access, never your Google password.

Referral Dash's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not transferred to anyone else, not read by people, and not used to train artificial intelligence models.

4. Sharing

Reports you upload follow the sharing settings of the Drive folder you picked, so anyone with access to that folder can see them. Only pick a folder shared with people who may see this information. The app does not change sharing settings or create public links.

The app communicates directly from your computer with Tableau and with Google. Those services handle information under their own policies. Vercel hosts this website and may process ordinary request details, such as IP address and browser, to deliver it. This website sets no cookies and loads no advertising or analytics.

5. Keeping and deleting information

The app keeps the newest report sets on your computer, 8 by default, and deletes older ones. In Drive it keeps only the newest copy and moves the previous one to the Drive trash. You can change that number, or remove reports yourself at any time.

To remove the app's data from your computer, delete your reports folder and %LOCALAPPDATA%\Referral Dash, and remove the "Referral Dash" entries from Windows Credential Manager. Reports already uploaded stay in Drive until you delete them.

6. Disconnecting Google

Click Disconnect in the app to remove its Google connection from your computer. To revoke its access entirely, go to your Google Account connections. Neither step deletes reports already in your Drive.
