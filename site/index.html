Referral Dash

Weekly mission reports in one click.

A Windows app that turns a mission's Tableau referral data into dashboard pictures and district summaries, showing how each area changed from an earlier period.

How it works ↓

It runs on your computer.

Reports are made and saved on your own PC. Nothing is sent to the people who make Referral Dash.

Signs in to Tableau with your accountSaves reports in a folder you chooseOptionally uploads to one Google Drive folder

How it works

From referral data to shared reports.

  1. 01

    Download

    The app signs in to Tableau with the account you saved and downloads your mission's referral list for the dates you set.

  2. 02

    Report

    It makes area, district, zone and mission dashboards, plus district summaries showing growth or decline.

  3. 03

    Share

    Pictures are saved on your PC. If you connect Google Drive, they also upload to the one folder you pick.

Google Drive

Access to one folder, not your Drive.

Connecting is optional. Referral Dash uses Google's narrowest Drive permission, so it can only reach the folder you pick and the reports it put there. It cannot see or change anything else in your Drive.
