Using the app

Terms of service

Effective November 6, 2026

Referral Dash is a reporting tool for people authorized to work with their mission's referral data. These terms describe appropriate use.

1. Authorized use

Use the app only with accounts and data you are authorized to access, and follow your organization's policies and the terms of the services it connects to, including Tableau and Google. Don't use it to get around access restrictions, to use someone else's account without permission, or to collect information unrelated to your reporting work.

2. Your responsibilities

Reports can include the names of real people. You are responsible for where you save them and who can see them, including the sharing settings of any Google Drive folder you pick. Protect your Windows account, since the app stores its connections there.

3. What the app does in Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, each run puts its folders of pictures inside the folder you picked and moves the copy it uploaded the previous time to the Drive trash. Google may later remove trashed items permanently. The app does not touch anything else in your Drive.

4. Accuracy and availability

Reports depend on data from Tableau, which can be delayed or corrected later. Comparisons with earlier dates reflect that data as it is now, so they may not exactly match a report made at the time. Check the source records before relying on a report for an important decision.

The app is provided as is, without warranties. It may stop working if the services it connects to change, or if an administrator restricts access. Referral Dash is not made by or endorsed by Google or Tableau.

5. Stopping use

You can stop using the app at any time. The privacy policy explains how to disconnect Google and remove the app's data. Stopping does not remove reports already shared with others.

6. Changes and support

The app and these terms may change, and the date above identifies this version. For support or questions, email shmoogle212@gmail.com.
